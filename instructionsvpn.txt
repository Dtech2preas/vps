Step 3: Setup v2rayNG on Android
1. Download `v2rayNG` from the Google Play Store.
2. Open the app, click the "+" icon in the top right, and select "Type manually (VLESS)".
3. Fill in the following details EXACTLY like this:
   - Remarks: Free MTN
   - Address (server): 104.16.248.249 (or onlinecms.mtn.co.za)
   - Port: 443
   - ID (UUID): 611100ce-f8d4-4a5e-89db-fded7bfb1816
   - Flow: (Leave blank)
   - Network: ws (WebSocket)
   - Security: tls
   - Path: /
   - Host: zero.secure-login.co.za
   - SNI: onlinecms.mtn.co.za
   - Fingerprint (fp): chrome
4. Save the configuration (the checkmark in the top right).
5. Tap the "V" icon at the bottom to connect.

Your carrier will see a TLS connection going directly to onlinecms.mtn.co.za (zero-rated). The traffic will actually hit Cloudflare, where your Worker on zero.secure-login.co.za will unwrap it and give you full, free internet access!
